## Brand & Style
The design system embodies an ultra-minimalist, high-performance dark aesthetic engineered specifically for high-end automotive rental web applications and mobile-first PWAs. Drawing inspiration from modern precision frameworks (Inspora, Uiarc, BeUI), the tone is restrained, technical, and stealthy. 

The aesthetic is grounded in absolute visual discipline: deep matte black voids, precision low-contrast boundaries, and razor-sharp typographic hierarchies. Rather than relying on skeuomorphism or loud decorative surfaces, it relies on strict proportions, disciplined micro-interactions, tactile mobile touch targets, and deliberate focal accents. The emotional response is one of effortless luxury, high-octane engineering reliability, and immediate transactional clarity.

## Colors
The palette is architected around a layered obsidian foundation. Pure surfaces progress from `surface-base` (`#0B0C0E`) through `surface-raised` (`#121316`) to `surface-card` (`#181A1F`), framing visual content without stark breaks. Outlines rely on a deliberate hairline value (`#2A2E37`).

The primary chromatic element is an electric lime accent (`#D8F944`). This color is preserved strictly for terminal actions: primary booking conversions, thumb-activated bottom-bar triggers, and critical state progression. Secondary interfaces default to crisp monochrome tones: stark white (`#FFFFFF`) for headers and prominent vehicle specs, paired with structural slate grays (`#717786`) for metadata and micro-labels. Status badges utilize subdued, pastel-tinted chromatic tokens on deep, desaturated translucent backgrounds, preventing chromatic noise across vehicle catalogues and rental dashboards.

## Typography
Typographic rules strictly balance technical precision and glanceable mobile legibility:
- **Display & Headlines:** Set in Geist for its razor-sharp geometric aperture, high x-height, and architectural neutrality.
- **Body:** Rendered in Inter for frictionless paragraph readability, clear disambiguation of characters, and predictable vertical metrics.
- **Tabular Figures & Currency:** All numeric financial outputs (e.g., `R$ 1.250,00`, mileage, duration) enforce OpenType tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) using Geist to guarantee alignment across tabular reservation breakdowns.
- **Date Badges & Sub-labels:** Formatted in uppercase or tracked lowercase (e.g., `03 nov`), applying `label-badge` with generous tracking to sustain high visibility even against dense dark cards.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model is tailored for a mobile viewport baseline of 390px width (iPhone / modern mobile flagship scale), with seamless responsive expansion to tablet (768px) and desktop (1280px+).

- **Mobile Rhythm (390px):** Single-column layout framed by a constant `margin` of 16px (`1rem`) and a strict 4-column micro-grid with 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters. Stacked content utilizes vertical element spacing governed by `space-sm` (8px), `space-md` (12px), and `space-lg` (16px).
- **Desktop/Tablet Expansion:** Expands to an 8-column layout on tablet and a 12-column fixed grid (max-width 1200px) on desktop, shifting `margin-desktop` to 40px and `gutter-desktop` to 24px.
- **Mobile Ergonomics:** The bottom 88px of the viewport is designated as an thumb-accessible floating dock for transactional navigation, vehicle booking summary, and conversion CTAs.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is expressed through tonal stepping and subtle boundary definition rather than ambient drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Grounded at `#0B0C0E`. Zero elevation, non-interactive floor.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Lists):** Grounded at `#181A1F` with a 1px uniform hairline border of `#2A2E37`. No blur or shadow; physical depth is derived entirely from value contrast.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Modules / Menus):** Grounded at `#1F2228` with a 1px border of `#2A2E37` and an ambient drop shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Floating Bottom Dock):** Grounded in translucent backdrop acrylics: `#121316` at 85% opacity, paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and an upper hairline stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. This creates separation over dynamic car galleries.

## Shapes
Geometry is disciplined, modern, and ergonomic:
- **Cards & Enclosures:** Use standard radius tokens (12px to 16px) to frame vehicle photography, checkout summaries, and calendar selectors smoothly.
- **Controls & Input Fields:** Standardized at 12px border-radius, maintaining structural alignment with card bounds.
- **Interactive Badges & Meta Chips:** Fully rounded pills (`border-radius: 9999px`) to create clear geometric contrast against boxy vehicle spec cards.
- **Stroke Widths:** Constrained strictly to 1px for element borders, dividers, and icon line weights, preserving an engineering-grade silhouette.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Styled in `#D8F944` with deep black `#0B0C0E` typography at 600 weight. Minimum height of 48px to satisfy touch-target requirements. Active state triggers a subtle scale compression (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background with a 1px border in `#2A2E37`, using `#FFFFFF` text. Hover or focus transitions border to `#717786`.
- **Bottom Navigation Dock:** Floating translucent pill or pinned bottom sheet featuring the primary CTA alongside real-time calculated total (`label-numeric`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges (Open, Pending, Overdue, Paid):** Rendered as compact pill containers (height 24px) utilizing the respective desaturated background and saturated foreground tokens (e.g., `#12231A` background with `#4ADE80` text for Open/Active rentals).
- **Date Badges:** Compact rectangular badges (height 28px, 6px radius) in `#1F2228` with `#FFFFFF` text, housing condensed date labels (e.g., `03 NOV`).

### Vehicle Display Cards
- High-contrast charcoal container (`#181A1F`), crisp border (`#2A2E37`), housing an edge-to-edge transparent car render.
- Metrics grid: Tabular numbers showing specs (0-100 km/h, range, power) separated by thin dividers (`#1F2228`).
- Price readout anchored to bottom-right: `label-numeric` formatting `R$ 1.250,00` accompanied by `/dia` in `body-sm` muted slate.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#121316`. Border: 1px `#2A2E37`. Typography: `#FFFFFF`. Placeholder: `#717786`.
- Focused state transitions border to white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px hairline glow (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`). Minimum height of 48px with inner horizontal padding of 16px.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- Custom 20px squares or pill toggles. Unchecked: `#1F2228` with `#2A2E37` border. Checked: filled with primary electric accent (`#D8F944`), displaying a high-contrast black check mark or thumb.

### Lists & Activity Rows
- Grouped inside unified cards. Rows separated by 1px horizontal borders (`#1F2228`). Interactive rows feature chevron indicators rendered in `#717786` and touch-feedback ripple/surface brightening to `#1F2228`.
